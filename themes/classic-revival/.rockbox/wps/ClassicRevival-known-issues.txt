Classic Revival - known issues
==============================

Inherited from iClassic 1.1 (Humberto Santana):

The perspective effect on the Now Playing album art is an illusion: the
square cover is covered by a "perspective frame", and the reflection is a
fixed image. It works on most covers, but:

1. When a track starts, the art can show square for a moment before the
   frame is drawn.
2. Very dark or near-white covers can look a little "scratchy" along the
   top and bottom edges.
3. Covers with strong horizontal lines, frames or borders can break the
   illusion.

New in Classic Revival:

4. The ">" chevrons are chosen from the icon Rockbox gives each list item,
   so a few items differ from stock (for example the entries in the System
   menu have none, because Rockbox treats them as actions).
5. Dialogs shown while the main menu is open (for example "Database is not
   ready") are drawn inside the left half, because the skin cannot tell them
   apart from the menu itself.
6. "Database" is renamed to "Music" (as on stock) on the main menu item and
   the database browser's title only; the Database entry in Settings >
   General Settings keeps its name. Screen titles are translated through
   Rockbox's language files, but "Unknown Artist" and "Unknown Album" on Now
   Playing are always in English, as Rockbox has no translation for them.
7. The soft shadow at the edge of the artwork pane is not drawn over album
   art (Rockbox blends transparent images against the backdrop, not against
   the album art).
8. On the iPod Video the FM Radio screen needs the iPod Radio Remote, which
   cannot report signal strength, so no signal meter is shown.

Fixed in Classic Revival: menu text being clipped after a track change, and
the battery icon disappearing on Now Playing at 96-100% charge.
